FAQ

What people ask us most.

What profile are we looking for in Algeria?

Entrepreneurs or investor groups with business vision, the capacity to build a national structure and, ideally, experience in real estate, franchising or managing sales teams.

Do I need real estate experience?

It helps, but it isn't essential. Head Office gives you manuals, training and support, and your Quartz pilot office lets you learn and validate the model in Algeria before scaling it.

What does the exclusivity include?

The right to market the Realtyplus brand and its franchises across Algeria for 10 years, including selling Area Masters by province or region.

How are franchise prices set in Algeria?

The standard values of the international business model apply. Any adjustment or change must be agreed with the International Head Office and backed by a detailed study of income per capita, competition and population in the country.
